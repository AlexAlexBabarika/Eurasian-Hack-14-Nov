Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

Notely Ltd ("Notely", "we", "us") provides a note-taking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information we collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data: your email address, your name and password when you create an account; the content you submit to the service, including your notes and chat messages; payment and billing details when you subscribe; and usage information, device identifiers and your IP address when you use our website.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to provide and maintain the service, to authenticate you, to take payments, to understand how the service is used, to find and fix errors, and to respond to your support requests.

4. Service providers

We share personal data with service providers who process it on our behalf and only on our instructions: Mixpanel and PostHog (product analytics; email, name, usage, device identifiers, IP address), Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (website analytics; usage, device identifiers, IP address), Sentry (error monitoring; email, usage, device identifiers, IP address), Stripe (payments; email, payment details) and OpenAI (AI features; the content you submit). We do not sell your personal data.

5. Artificial intelligence

Some features of our service use artificial intelligence. When you use them, the content you submit is sent to OpenAI (based in the United States), which processes it to generate a response.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside the UK, the European Economic Area and Kazakhstan, mainly in the United States. When we make these international transfers of personal data we rely on standard contractual clauses.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in. We use Google Analytics and PostHog cookies to measure how our website is used; we only set them after you give consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time in cookie settings.

8. Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active and delete it within 30 days after you close it. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

9. Your rights

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and the right to erasure. You can delete your account and personal data at any time from Settings, Account, or by emailing privacy@notely.example.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@notely.example.
